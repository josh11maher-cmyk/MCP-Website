Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does my generator need maintenance?
We recommend a full service maintenance visit at least once a year, or after every 100 hours of run time. This ensures your oil is clean, filters are fresh, and the battery is ready for an emergency.
Do you service generators that you didn't install?
Yes, absolutely! We service and repair **any** make or model of generator, regardless of who originally installed it. Our certified technicians are trained to handle a wide variety of systems.
How quickly can you respond to an emergency?
We offer 24/7 emergency repair services. For contract customers, we have guaranteed response times. For all other emergencies, we prioritize critical safety calls and aim to be on-site as rapidly as possible.
What areas of Michigan do you serve?
We are based in Byron Center and serve the greater West Michigan area, including Grand Rapids, Holland, Kalamazoo, and surrounding communities. Contact us to see if you are in our coverage zone.
